FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is my Medical ID generated?

A unique Medical ID and matching QR code are generated automatically the moment you register as a patient.

Who can see my records?

Only you, the doctors you've been treated by, and hospitals you've granted permission to. Every view is logged.

What happens in an emergency?

Authorized emergency doctors can scan your QR code to see only critical information — blood group, allergies, current medications, chronic conditions, and your emergency contact. You're notified every time this happens.

How do doctors and hospitals join?

Doctors and hospital administrators register and are reviewed and approved by a system administrator before they can log in.
