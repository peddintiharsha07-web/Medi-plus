About the platform

Built to make a patient's history follow them, not their paperwork.

MedGrid is a centralized digital medical record management system. It connects patients, doctors, hospital administrators, and system administrators on a single secure platform — so a patient's diagnosis history, prescriptions, and lab reports are one QR scan away, no matter which registered hospital they walk into.

◈

Our mission

Remove the friction of fragmented paper records and give every patient one continuous digital health identity.

⚿

Security first

Role-based access control, encrypted credentials, and a full audit trail on every sensitive action — including emergency access.

⟲

Built for scale

A modular Django REST architecture supports multi-hospital growth, multilingual users, and thousands of records.
